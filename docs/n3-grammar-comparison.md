# N3 grammar PDF comparison

The imported chapter sequence is numbered 1–19, including Chapter 1 (Time and Order) and Chapter 2 (Relationships and Change). All active N3 Grammar cards use the existing Wide (3 cards) layout. The presentation update was saved and verified at database revision 212, preserving study content and owner deletions.

Reviewed on 2026-10-05. Both supplied PDFs are image scans: Shinkanzen has 188 PDF pages; Minna has 171. The second book is Minna no Nihongo Intermediate I (中級 I).

Shinkanzen organizes grammar by meaning, form, sentence structure and connected text. Minna teaches overlapping grammar through 12 lessons and adds conversational contractions, compound verbs and practical interaction patterns. The combined catalog groups related patterns by topic, keeping different meanings of the same spelling distinct.

The catalog contains 146 bilingual study cards in 19 chapters. 48 cards have references to both books; 75 reference Shinkanzen only and 23 reference Minna only. These are card-level references for the selected lesson headings and index entries, not a count of every occurrence in either book. Related variants share a card where appropriate. Earlier-level foundations are retained when taught or reviewed in these books.

Every card uses the existing Grammar schema: pattern and Myanmar meaning, Japanese example with readings, Myanmar translation, and connection rules plus Myanmar explanation. Explanations and examples are newly written; scanned textbook passages are not copied into the site. Existing owner cards are retained.

Source references use printed book pages for Shinkanzen and one-based PDF pages for Minna. Shinkanzen scan offsets vary, so do not compute PDF pages using a fixed offset. The uploaded card metadata also retains these references.

## Maintenance

The editable source is `source/n3-grammar.tsv`; `migrations/grammarCatalog.js` validates it and constructs cards in the existing format. The frontend continues to load its content exclusively from Firebase.

`migrations/upload-grammar.mjs <firebase-tools-package-directory>` performs a read-only comparison and writes local backups under ignored `.migration-backups/`. Add `--apply` to import. It preserves owner edits and deletions, skips existing cards, checks the database ETag, increments the revision, and verifies the result. Only `/jlpt/content` is written. N2 is read for a before/after check and is never written.
